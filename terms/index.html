Assessments and unknown data

Compatibility scores are deterministic comparisons of the data currently available in the app with dimensions you enter. Missing values are treated as unknown, and user-entered or third-party values may be wrong. A high score does not mean a place has been inspected, approved, measured, or verified and does not predict safe access.

AR tracking and surface placement are approximate and depend on the device, lighting, texture, motion, and visible environment. Pitch Preview cannot see hidden hazards or reliably determine overhead clearance, soil strength, traction, mud depth, holes, roots, rocks, slope, legal boundaries, turning room, approach/departure angles, or safe vehicle access. The 2D planner is a dimensioned diagram, not a site measurement.

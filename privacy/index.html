7 Miles: Camper AR

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: September 5, 2026

7 Miles is designed to work without an account, advertising, third-party analytics, or a developer-operated backend. The developer does not directly receive app data. Hosted service operators may process the limited data described below when you use network features.

Information kept on your iPhone

7 Miles stores the following in its local app container:

  • your rig nickname, type, drivetrain, dimensions, clearance, and optional awning configuration;
  • saved field packs, including selected place names, address context, coordinates, source summaries, and assessment inputs;
  • private candidates you add, including a name, short note, coordinates, observed surface, optional access and pitch dimensions, grade, 4×4 requirement, overnight status, evidence note, and the date you say the information was observed or checked; and
  • whether onboarding has been completed.
  • trip preferences, your chosen overnight stop and backup, up to three service stops, manually entered water/waste/battery levels, capacities, daily consumption and expected solar assumptions;
  • a downloaded trip pack, including OpenStreetMap road geometry, place labels, stop details and the available weather forecast. The pack is stored in Application Support and excluded from device backup. Ordinary network response caches may also be held on the device.

This data is used only to provide app functionality. It is not sent to a server controlled by the 7 Miles developer and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Location

Location is optional and first activated when you choose nearby results. While permission remains granted, the app may refresh a stale when-in-use fix on launch or foreground to keep nearby results current. It uses that fix on device to calculate distance and centre place searches. Before querying hosted OpenStreetMap Overpass or MET Norway services, 7 Miles rounds the search centre to two decimal places (roughly one-kilometre resolution). The raw GPS fix is not sent to OpenStreetMap, MET Norway or a developer server. 7 Miles does not request background location, create a trip history, or publish your position.

Camera and augmented reality

Camera access is requested only after you start the stationary Pitch Preview and confirm that the vehicle is parked and the area is safe to walk. Apple’s ARKit and RealityKit frameworks process camera frames and motion/ tracking state to place the visual footprint. 7 Miles does not save or upload camera frames.

Place search, Apple Maps, and OpenStreetMap

The native map display, transient typed-area resolution, current-location services, requested driving-time/detour estimates, directions handoff, and the iOS share sheet are Apple system services. Apple may process search, location, map, diagnostics, or sharing information under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The 7 Miles developer does not receive that information through a developer server.

For broader camper-place coverage, the app sends an approximate search centre directly to the FOSSGIS-operated main Overpass service, with Private.coffee as a fallback, and receives community-maintained place records. Service searches and explicit offline road-map downloads use the same providers and rounded coordinates. A typed town or area is resolved before the approximate centre is sent. Those operators receive normal connection metadata and may keep operational logs under their policies. Private.coffee says it truncates IP addresses and retains its request metadata for up to 48 hours. The request does not include an account or advertising identifier. See the OpenStreetMap copyright and licence page, the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy, and Private.coffee’s usage and privacy policy. 7 Miles does not send a rig profile, saved field pack, private note, advertising identifier, account ID, or camera frame with a place-search request. Place records come only from OpenStreetMap contributors; Apple place results are not stored or merged into the inventory.

Weather forecasts

When you use Tonight with a chosen stop or search area, 7 Miles requests a forecast and sunset information from MET Norway over HTTPS. The service receives a two-decimal location and ordinary connection data, including your IP address. MET Norway states that IP addresses and requested coordinates may appear in logs stored in Norway. No supply levels, rig profile, notes, account identifier or camera data are sent. Forecasts are cached, marked with their retrieval time, and may be included in a trip pack. There is no background weather polling. See MET API terms and MET Norway privacy.

Supply estimates and downloaded maps

Supply calculations run on your iPhone from information you enter. The app does not connect to tank or battery sensors. Offline road geometry is derived from OpenStreetMap under ODbL. Apple map tiles and Apple driving responses are not included in offline packs. You can delete the download separately in Trip Pack. My Rig → Delete local data clears the pack, trip, supplies, preferences, saved places and rig information.

External links and support

If you open a support, source, privacy, terms, or other external link, your browser or destination service may receive normal connection data and any information you choose to provide. GitHub hosts this support site and may process standard web-service logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. Email and public GitHub issues are not part of the app and should not be used to send sensitive location or travel information.

Tracking, advertising, and analytics

7 Miles contains no advertising, attribution, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not combine app data with third-party data for tracking.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you change or delete it, reset local data from My Rig, or uninstall the app. “Delete local data” clears the rig profile, saved field packs, supplies, chosen trip, preferences, downloaded maps and onboarding state. Because there is no 7 Miles account or developer backend, there is no server-side account record to delete.

Children

7 Miles is a general travel utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly solicit personal data from children. Network services process the limited request data described above.

Security

Local data receives the protections provided by iOS and your device. No method of storage is risk-free, so protect access to your iPhone and avoid placing sensitive information in public support requests.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed feature is released. The date above identifies the current policy version.

7 Miles: Camper AR for iPhone

Your next 24 hours on the road.

Choose tonight’s stop and a backup, find water and waste services, track your supplies, and take a regional road map and your essential stop details offline.

⚠︎
Planning aid, not a guarantee. Conditions, signs, access, and rules can change. Verify the site on arrival and never use Pitch Preview while driving.

Quick start

Three useful first steps

1

Add your rig

Open My Rig and enter the vehicle dimensions, drivetrain, ground clearance, and optional awning. These details stay in local app storage on your iPhone.

2

Search an area

Use Explore to search a town or campground. Location is optional. Results come from OpenStreetMap contributors, are shown on a native Apple map, and must be checked against local signs and official information.

3

Plan tonight and download

Choose an option in Tonight, add a backup and service stops, then download your Trip Pack. Find Pitch Preview from My Rig or place details when you are safely parked.

Help

Common questions

Why are so many dimensions “Unknown”?

Map place records generally do not include camper access width, height, pitch size, grade, or permission to stay overnight. 7 Miles keeps those values unknown instead of guessing. Unknown data lowers confidence.

Can I add a place from another map or trail plan?

Yes. Tap the plus button in Explore and enter a name and coordinates. You may also record optional dimensions, grade, surface, 4×4 requirement, overnight evidence, and the date you checked it. The candidate is stored only in this app on your iPhone. These are personal notes: adding them does not establish road access, landowner permission, or overnight legality.

Does a score mean a place is safe or legal?

No. A score only compares the information currently available in the app with your local rig profile. It cannot confirm current road conditions, permission, signage, barriers, clearance, ground stability, or safe access.

Can I use 7 Miles as an offline map?

Yes. Choose a primary stop, backup and service stops, then use Trip Pack to download regional road-map areas. Each area has a 10 km radius; nearby stops can share an area. Roads, tracks, place labels, selected stop details and the available forecast are stored on your iPhone. Open the downloaded pack before leaving reception to check its coverage. Satellite imagery, Apple map tiles and turn-by-turn routing are not downloaded. New searches and driving estimates need a connection.

How do supply estimates work?

Set your water, waste and battery levels from Tonight. Enter capacities and expected daily use for everyone travelling. Estimates account for elapsed time and retain 10% fresh water, 10% empty waste capacity and 20% battery as reserves. Expected solar is your own input; leave it at zero for a conservative estimate. Update actual levels daily. No tank or battery sensor is connected.

Can I trust a service point or driving estimate?

Service records show community map facts and their edit date, not proof that a tap or disposal point works today. Check posted hours, access and accepted waste type. LPG listings do not establish bottle-refill or adapter compatibility. Apple driving and detour estimates use ordinary car routes; they do not check your camper dimensions.

Where does weather come from?

Forecasts and sunset information are from MET Norway, summarized for the area of your selected stop or search. Cached forecasts show their retrieval time; refresh after reconnecting. Sunset is displayed in your iPhone’s time zone.

Why does Pitch Preview ask me to confirm I am parked?

The camera feature is designed only for stationary, on-foot use. Secure the vehicle, check that it is safe and permitted to walk in the area, and stay aware of traffic, terrain, and other hazards.

The camera or AR preview is unavailable.

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and check access for 7 Miles. AR also requires a supported iPhone and enough textured, well-lit horizontal ground for tracking. The 2D planner remains available without AR.

Nearby results are unavailable.

Location is optional. Search by town or area instead, or open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → 7 Miles and allow access while using the app. 7 Miles does not request background location.

How do I delete my data?

Open My Rig → Delete local data and confirm. This clears the local rig, saved places, supply levels, trip preferences, chosen stops, downloaded pack and onboarding state. Uninstalling also removes the app container. There is no 7 Miles account or developer cloud record.

Accessibility

7 Miles uses native iOS controls, labels icon-only actions, and offers list and 2D alternatives to its map and camera-based planning views. AR inspection remains a visual, on-foot aid. Please tell us if you encounter a barrier.
